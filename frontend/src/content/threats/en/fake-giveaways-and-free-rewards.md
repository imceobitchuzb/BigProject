The desire for rare skins, premium Battle Passes, and Discord Nitro makes gamers receptive to giveaway announcements. Scammers exploit this anticipation using social media automation and rogue chat bots.

## 1. Malicious QR Code Authorization Hijacking

Discord and gaming platforms introduced QR codes to make logging into console or television apps convenient. Scammers weaponized this feature:
- A bot posts an image in a server: *"Claim 3 Free Months of Discord Nitro! Scan this official Discord QR code with your mobile app!"*
- **What is actually happening:** The QR code is NOT a gift redemption link. It is an **instant authentication token** for the attacker's server.
- The moment you scan that code using Discord's camera scan tool, your session token is transferred directly to the attacker. They gain full access to your account without needing your password or 2FA code.
- **Rule:** Only scan QR codes displayed on your own personal device (e.g. your television screen or desktop monitor) that you personally initiated.

## 2. Hijacked Influencer Livestreams

Scammers routinely purchase or compromise YouTube channels with millions of subscribers, rename the channel to prominent esports figures (like s1mple, Shroud, or official studio accounts), and broadcast looped video footage.
- A prominent overlay promises free knife giveaways or game keys.
- Live chat is disabled to prevent viewers from exposing the fraud.
- Viewers who follow the link are prompted to connect their gaming inventories or pay a "deposit" to receive the prize.
- Always check the official verified social channels (X/Twitter, official Discord) of publishers before interacting with any broadcast giveaway.
